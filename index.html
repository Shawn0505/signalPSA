PROJECT SUMMARY

Concept, Scope, and Intent

SignalPSA is a conceptual, non‑invasive urine biomarker monitoring system that explores earlier prostate health risk awareness through microfluidic biosensors, AI signal processing, and longitudinal trend analysis. It is not a diagnostic device; it’s a design study showing how continuous, passive monitoring could complement clinical care.

Today’s pathways (PSA blood tests, MRI, biopsies) are invasive, episodic, and costly—so weak biological shifts can be missed between appointments. By passively sampling urine at home, a smart attachment and disposable microfluidic cartridge could detect PSA‑related proteins, PCA3, inflammation markers, and other signals. Edge AI normalizes dilution, filters noise, and compares multi‑marker trends to a personalized baseline to surface unusual patterns that may warrant clinical follow‑up.

The aim is to illustrate a shift from reactive, visit‑based medicine toward preventative, personalized monitoring—where early signals appear in everyday life, not only in the lab.
